    BEHIND THE MAGIC

    Small model.
    Clear thinking.

    SignalGarden uses a real Naive Bayes text classifier. It learns which words tend to appear in each category, then estimates where a new thought belongs.

    The highlighted words show what influenced its top prediction. Predictions are estimates, so give it more examples when it gets something wrong.
